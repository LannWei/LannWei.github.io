👋About Me

I am a PhD student in Robotics at Imperial College London, advised by Dr Dandan Zhang. I work on embodied AI: building robots that combine vision, touch and language to perceive, reason and act in the physical world.

My research spans vision-language-action models and multimodal-LLM agents for manipulation, visuo-tactile representation learning, and diffusion-based generative models, deployed on real robots from cluttered tabletop scenes to automated laboratory experiments and optical microrobots. I also co-organise workshops on world models for embodied AI at ECCV and IROS.

Before Imperial, I received my M.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), working on physics-informed graph neural networks, and my B.Eng. from Xiamen University.

🤖Research Highlights

  • Robot foundation models and multimodal-LLM agents. Grounding language in perception for safe, reliable manipulation: VLA post-training on robot demonstrations, ambiguity-aware language-guided manipulation, constrained multimodal-LLM agents that respect physical limits, and LLM-based scheduling for multi-robot teams. [RefGuard] [ICRA26] [MARSS26] [IROS26 WS] [Multi-robot]
  • Visuo-tactile perception and representation learning. Treating touch as a first-class modality for robot learning: a cross-sensor tactile dataset and benchmark, self-supervised visuo-tactile representations, few-shot adaptation to unseen sensors, and LLM-driven architecture search for tactile models. [ARR26] [ICRA26] [IROS26] [BIFTA]
  • Robotics for science: lab automation and microrobots. Perception and control for automating physical experiments: multi-pipette localisation for robot-assisted in vivo patch-clamp, pose and depth estimation of optical microrobots, sim-to-real data generation, and shared-control and digital-twin teleoperation for micromanipulation. [IROS25] [MARSS25] [CBS25] [ICRA26] [MARSS26]
  • Generative models and physics-informed learning. Diffusion models that synthesise tactile and microscopy data, faster and more stable diffusion-LLM decoding, efficient Vision Transformers, and physics-informed graph networks as learned simulators for fluids and Arctic sea ice. [ICML26] [ICRA26] [ICLR24] [JGR25] [TVC25]
